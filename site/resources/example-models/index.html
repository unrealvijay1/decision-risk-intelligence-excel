Example Model Recipes

Build these small models directly in Excel. These are instructions, not downloadable workbooks.

Budget uncertainty

Put quantity in A2 and unit cost in B2, then =A2*B2 in C2. Define suitable positive distributions for the two inputs, mark C2 as a forecast and calculate the probability of staying below your budget. Use P80 to explore a contingency level.

Revenue planning

Use price in A2, demand in B2 and =A2*B2 in C2. Compare alternative demand assumptions through Scenario Analysis. If demand depends on price, express that relationship in the workbook instead of treating the cells as unrelated.

Resource allocation

Place allocations in decision cells and calculate profit, total labor and total material through formulas. Add bounds and worksheet constraints in Optimizer. Review feasibility and explicitly apply the selected candidate.

Process stability

Place observations in time order in one column, with labels in another. Choose SPC Analysis and an initial baseline. Append later observations and examine signals against the fixed reference limits.
